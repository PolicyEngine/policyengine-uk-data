Calibrate households' SDLT (`stamp_duty_land_tax`) to the OBR's SDLT forecast less policyengine-uk's corporate SDLT statistic for the same year, instead of the whole OBR line. policyengine-uk adds SDLT paid by companies and other non-natural persons separately, as `corporate_sdlt`, so calibration counted that share twice: on the 1.58.0 dataset with policyengine-uk 2.122.2, households' SDLT was £16.32bn in 2025-26 against the OBR's £15.41bn for all buyers, and `corporate_sdlt` added £3.13bn on top. With policyengine-uk 2.152.0 or later, which projects the corporate statistic with the OBR forecast (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2322), the 2025-26 household target is £10.60bn. Full builds now need such a version and stop at the start with an earlier one, which holds the statistic at £3.13bn in every year.
