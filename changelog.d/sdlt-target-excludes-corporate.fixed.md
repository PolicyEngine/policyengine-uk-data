Calibrate households' SDLT (`stamp_duty_land_tax`) to the OBR's SDLT forecast less policyengine-uk's corporate SDLT statistic for the same year, instead of the whole OBR line. policyengine-uk adds SDLT paid by companies and other non-natural persons separately, as `corporate_sdlt`, so calibration counted that share twice: on the 1.58.0 dataset with PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2322, 2026-27 SDLT came to £23.29bn (£17.31bn households, £5.98bn corporate) against the OBR's £18.15bn. The 2026-27 household target becomes £12.17bn. Full builds now need a policyengine-uk with #2322, which projects the corporate statistic with the OBR forecast, and stop at the start with an earlier one, which holds it at £3.13bn in every year.
