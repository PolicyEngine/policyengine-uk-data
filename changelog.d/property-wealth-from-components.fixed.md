Stop saving the WAS-imputed `property_wealth` column. policyengine-uk defines `property_wealth` as `main_residence_value + other_residential_property_value + non_residential_property_value`, but a saved column overrides that sum and has no uprating index, so it stayed at its 2024 value while the components were uprated: on release 1.57.4 it was identical per household in 2024, 2025 and 2026, and in 2026 it was below `main_residence_value` for 29,677 of 35,672 owner households. The saved column was not the sum of the components even in 2024 (£8,126.9bn against £8,934.1bn weighted; different for 12,476 of 52,846 households). The WAS total is still imputed, because the later wealth outputs are predicted conditional on it, but it is no longer written, so `property_wealth`, `household_land_value`, `land_value` and `total_wealth` now follow the uprated components. The household and regional land targets are unchanged, but the land the calibration fits to them now comes from the components: at 1.57.4's weights, 2025 household land is £5,752.3bn rather than £5,165.4bn against a £5,042.9bn target, so the next build's weights will move.
