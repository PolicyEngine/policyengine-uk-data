- FRS investment-account dividends are now summed to the person who holds the account. They were keyed on each person's row position, so almost none matched and the base FRS carried about £40m of dividends instead of about £8bn in 2024-25.
- The enhanced FRS keeps those reported dividends instead of replacing them with draws from the SPI income model, which predicts from age, gender and region alone and gave Universal Credit claimants six-figure dividends (policyengine-uk#1948). The SPI-donor half still carries the SPI income distribution.
