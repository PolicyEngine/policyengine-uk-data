Set `income_not_otherwise_charged` to FRS odd-job income, as a proxy for income charged under ITTOIA 2005 Part 5 Chapter 8, so policyengine-uk's trading allowance relieves it. The other parts of `miscellaneous_income` (royalties, a sleeping partner's share, overseas pensions, a child's spare-time earnings, trust income and an organisation's allowance) stay outside it, and the SPI-synthetic copy holds none.
