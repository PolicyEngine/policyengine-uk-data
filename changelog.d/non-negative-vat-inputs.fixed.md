- Stop the consumption and VAT imputations producing negative VAT. On 1.58.0, 76 households had negative VAT: 44 because an imputed housing, water and electricity amount was negative and outweighed their other consumption, and 32 because their imputed full-rate VAT expenditure rate was negative. Both came from survey training rows, whose range the imputation models' predictions stay within (9 LCFS households with a negative housing amount; 4 ETB households with expenditure at or below their VAT). The training tables now clamp consumption amounts at zero and keep the rate a share of expenditure, between 0 and 1, which also removes two households with rates above 1 (one of 55); the build fails if either property is violated; and cached models trained on the old tables are retrained.
