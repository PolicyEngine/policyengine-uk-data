Draw `would_claim_uc_at_legacy_closure` for benefit units that report a legacy means-tested benefit but not Universal Credit, at DWP's Move to Universal Credit claim rates for their combination of legacy benefits (Stat-Xplore, notices to end of March 2026). policyengine-uk reads it once one of those benefits closes, so a legacy reporter whose `would_claim_uc` draw was false now moves to Universal Credit at the observed rate instead of losing its legacy award with nothing in its place. Units reporting Universal Credit, and units with no legacy benefit, are true. The draw has its own seeded generator, so every other take-up flag is unchanged, and SPI-synthetic rows are redrawn after stage-2 imputation rewrites their benefit receipt (#492).
