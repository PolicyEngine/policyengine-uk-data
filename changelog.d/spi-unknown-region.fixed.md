Take Scottish taxpayer status in the SPI dataset from HMRC's `SCOT_TXP` flag rather than the region code. `GORCODE` 13, 14 and -1 (address abroad, address unknown, composite records) stay `UNKNOWN`, which policyengine-uk 2.104.5 and later can simulate; `load_spi_dataset` relabels them `SOUTH_EAST` only for older releases, and rebuilds a cached SPI H5 that predates the flag.
