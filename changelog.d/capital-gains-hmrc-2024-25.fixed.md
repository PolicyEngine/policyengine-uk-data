The 1.58.0 build targeted the 2023-24 all-taxpayer headline (£65.9bn, including trusts) and ended 19% under even that, with £53bn of gains above the exempt amount and a further -£15bn of losses on 1.4m people. Its calibration started 17 times over on CGT taxpayers, because the has-gains copy of each zero-weight SPI household received a full prior weight. Each household now splits its mass between itself and a has-gains twin by the Advani-Summers share for its income band, zero-weight households included; draws at or under the exempt amount, including losses, carry no gains; donors cover only the size bands from £500,000 up; and the calibration scales its starting weights so every HMRC size band's taxpayer count begins on target. The aggregate targets are now the sums of the size-band targets.
