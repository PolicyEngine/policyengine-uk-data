Code people on a government employment-training scheme as in approved training, from the FRS TRAINEE flag. The build read a `train` column that no FRS release has, so nobody was in approved training, and every FRS dependant aged 16 to 19 outside full-time education (all of them trainees) failed the education-or-training condition of the UC, Pension Credit, Child Benefit and Child Tax Credit qualifying-young-person tests.
